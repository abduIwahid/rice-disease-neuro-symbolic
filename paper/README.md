## Main paper — the one we implement

**Singh, C., Singh, A., & Dhelim, S. (2026).** "Neuro-symbolic AI for rice
disease diagnosis with calibrated attention and rule-aware explanations."
*Information Processing in Agriculture* (Elsevier), impact factor 8.9.
DOI: [10.1016/j.inpa.2026.02.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inpa.2026.02.006)

- File: [`singh-2026-rice-disease.pdf`](singh-2026-rice-disease.pdf)

  institutional repository.

## Shortlist alternatives — kept for reference

These two papers completed the three-paper shortlist presented to the
course instructor on 27 Nov 2026. They stay in the repo as references and
as fallbacks if the direction ever changes.

**Mondal, S., Ferraro, A., Pecorelli, F., Iammarino, M., & De Pietro, G.
(2026).** "Concept and rule guided neural network for early crop leaf
nutrient deficiency diagnosis." *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*
(Elsevier), impact factor 10.3.
DOI: [10.1016/j.compag.2026.111735](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2026.111735)

- File: [`mondal-2026-crop-nutrient-abstract.html`](mondal-2026-crop-nutrient-abstract.html)
  — the article is open access (CC BY); only the abstract page is saved
  here because ScienceDirect blocked the automated PDF download. The full
  text is one "View PDF" click on the article page.
- Kept because: the most faithful neuro-symbolic method of the three
  (concept bottleneck + fuzzy rules + KB); it becomes the first choice if
  its EarlyNSD dataset is confirmed downloadable.

**Hooshyar, D., Kikas, E., Yang, Y., Šír, G., Hämäläinen, R., Kärkkäinen,
T., & Azevedo, R. (2026).** "Towards responsible and trustworthy educational
data mining: Comparing symbolic, sub-symbolic, and neural-symbolic AI
methods." *International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education*
(Elsevier), impact factor 8.5 (2024 JCR).
DOI: [10.1016/j.ijaied.2026.100016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijaied.2026.100016)

- File: [`hooshyar-2026-education-kbann-preprint.pdf`](hooshyar-2026-education-kbann-preprint.pdf)
  — the authors' preprint (arXiv:2504.00615); the journal version is an
  accepted pre-proof with the same content.
- Kept because: the education-domain fallback (KBANN method); its own
  dataset is private, so an implementation would use a declared public
  substitute (UCI Student Performance / OULAD).
